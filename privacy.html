YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Draft updated: 4 November 2026

Draft for business review. This notice is not legally approved. Items marked “To confirm” must be settled before online enquiries are enabled. The online form currently does not send enquiries or photographs. You can contact John directly by phone or email.

Who we are

JohnsJobs is a clearance business based in Luton. For privacy questions, email john@johnsjobs.uk or call 07772 591409.

To confirm: the full legal identity of the data controller, business structure, correspondence address and any applicable registration details. “JohnsJobs” is the business name used on this website; this notice does not assume it is a registered company name.

Information covered by this notice

If you contact us by phone or email, your enquiry may include your name, contact details, location, the clearance work you need and information you choose to share. Once enabled, the online form will collect:

  • Full name, email address and phone number.
  • Job postcode or location, type of clearance and description of the job.
  • Up to five optional photographs to help explain what needs clearing.

Please avoid sending photos of people, personal paperwork or other sensitive information. A photo may reveal belongings, addresses or other personal details. If it includes someone else’s information, consider whether it is necessary to share it.

What happens while the form is disabled

Information you type into the form stays in the current page; this website does not send it to JohnsJobs or write it to local storage or session storage. Selected photos are previewed and resized on your device. This page does not upload them, and its in-memory selection is lost on reload. Your own browser may separately offer autofill or recovery features according to its settings.

Photos are converted to JPEG on a canvas, up to 1600 pixels on their longest edge, with a maximum prepared size of 1 MB each and 5 MB total. Original metadata is not copied by this conversion; details visible within the photo remain visible. Original images must be JPEG, PNG or WebP and no more than 5 MB each.

Choosing a phone or email link opens your device’s calling or email application. Information you choose to send through those applications is separate from the disabled online form.

Why information is used and the lawful basis

The intended enquiry workflow is to understand the job, respond to questions, discuss a quote and, where agreed, arrange a clearance. This notice does not assume a lawful basis has already been selected.

To confirm before launch: document the appropriate UK GDPR lawful basis for each purpose. Taking steps at an individual’s request before entering a contract may be relevant to a quote; legitimate interests may be relevant to certain business enquiries or records, subject to a documented assessment. Any legal-obligation purpose must identify an applicable obligation. These are candidates for review, not established bases. Decide how any unexpectedly received sensitive information will be handled.

How enquiries will be processed

The planned online workflow is for a secured backend to validate an enquiry, apply spam and rate limits, and send it to john@johnsjobs.uk. Resend is the intended email-delivery provider once the backend is implemented. It is not connected to this form today. No successful enquiry delivery should be inferred from entering information here.

To confirm: who can access enquiry mail, whether other systems are used, how enquiries are handled and deleted, and the lawful purposes of any business records. No marketing consent or analytics has been added to this form.

Hosting and other service providers

The website is currently published using GitHub Pages. Stylesheets request DM Sans and Manrope fonts from Google Fonts. Loading the site therefore involves requests to the host and, when available, Google’s font services. Those providers receive connection information such as IP address and request/browser information. Actual logging, retention and contractual roles must be checked with the providers.

Future online enquiries are intended to use Resend and either the client’s hosting backend or Cloudflare Workers; that backend choice remains undecided.

To confirm: the final host, email mailbox provider, any Cloudflare use, Resend arrangement, processor contracts, subprocessors, access controls and provider privacy documentation. We do not assume a data-processing agreement or a particular region is in place.

International transfers

Using hosting, font or email services can involve processing outside the UK. Provider names alone do not establish where information is processed or which safeguards apply.

To confirm: actual processing locations and transfer routes, and any applicable UK adequacy arrangements or appropriate contractual safeguards, such as the UK IDTA or UK Addendum where relevant, plus any required transfer assessment. Complete this review before enabling the backend.

How long information is kept

To confirm: specific retention periods or clear criteria for enquiries that do not become jobs, completed-job records, photos, mailbox copies, backups and technical/security logs. Set and document a deletion schedule based on operational needs and applicable obligations. No fixed retention period is asserted here.

Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, you may have rights to access your personal information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, restrict processing, object to processing or receive portable data. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it. Not every right applies in every situation.

To make a request, email john@johnsjobs.uk. Explain what you are requesting; identity or further details may be needed to handle it securely. Requests should be handled within applicable statutory time limits. You do not have to contact us first to complain to the regulator.

Complaints

You can raise concerns with John using the email above. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/.

Cookies and similar technologies

The current application code does not set cookies, use local storage or session storage, or include analytics, advertising pixels or embedded third-party widgets. Photo previews use temporary in-memory object URLs and a canvas to perform the photo preparation you request. Google Fonts requests are external requests; they should not be confused with an analytics integration.

The source audit found no current application technology that calls for a consent banner. Hosting/provider behaviour and final production responses still need checking. Reassess consent requirements if analytics, embedded content or device-accessing anti-spam technology is added. This is an audit conclusion for the current implementation, not a guarantee about all provider behaviour.

Changes to this notice

This draft will be updated after business details, providers and operational decisions are confirmed. Review the final notice before using the future online enquiry service.
