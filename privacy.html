YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Draft updated: 4 November 2026

Draft — awaiting confirmation. This notice includes the confirmed business details. The remaining points listed below must be resolved before publication as a final policy.

Who we are

JOHNSJOBS LTD, trading as JohnsJobs, is responsible for your personal information. For privacy questions or requests, email john@johnsjobs.uk.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide when you enquire about or use our services, including your name, email address, phone number, job address or location, clearance requirements and related correspondence. You may also choose to provide up to five photographs to help us understand the job.

Please avoid including people, private documents or sensitive information in photographs unless necessary. Contact details and job information are needed to respond to your request and arrange the service; photographs are optional.

Our website and its providers also receive connection information, such as your IP address and browser information, when you visit.

Why we use your information

We use customer information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, manage bookings and provide clearance services. We do not use it for marketing.

Proposed lawful bases — subject to confirmation:

  • Contract: taking steps you request before entering a contract, and providing services under that contract.
  • Legitimate interests: responding to enquiries made on behalf of a business, maintaining appropriate business records and handling queries or disputes. These interests must be balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation: keeping records required by applicable accounting and tax law, where relevant.

Who can access your information

Relevant customer information is accessible to authorised staff and our bookkeeper for the purposes described above.

Service providers covered by this notice include:

  • ServiceM8: customer and job management.
  • Resend: website enquiry email delivery.
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting.
  • Email providers: handling correspondence.
  • Google Fonts: supplying website fonts and receiving connection information when fonts are requested.

Provider roles, processing arrangements and international safeguards require the confirmation listed below.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. If the business is sold, transferred or reorganised, relevant information may be shared with advisers or a successor where lawful, with appropriate confidentiality and data-protection safeguards.

International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside the UK. Where this happens, a lawful transfer arrangement is required, such as UK adequacy regulations or appropriate contractual safeguards, together with any necessary assessment. The arrangements applying to our providers must be confirmed before this notice is finalised. You can ask us for details or a copy of applicable safeguards using the contact email above.

How long we keep information

Retention criteria — subject to confirmation: enquiry information should be kept for as long as needed to deal with the enquiry and any related follow-up. Booking and service records should be kept for as long as needed to provide the service, meet applicable record-keeping obligations and resolve complaints or legal claims. Photographs should be kept only while needed for those purposes.

Retention should take account of the record’s purpose, legal requirements and any ongoing dispute. Information should then be securely deleted or anonymised, including through appropriate arrangements for provider-held copies and backups. No fixed retention period is stated until the business confirms its requirements.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing or a portable copy. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Email john@johnsjobs.uk to make a request. We may need to verify your identity. Requests are normally answered within one month, with extensions permitted in certain circumstances. There is normally no charge. Some rights are subject to legal exceptions.

Complaints

Please contact us if you have a concern. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/. You do not need to contact us first.

Cookies and website services

The website does not currently include analytics or advertising trackers, and its application does not set cookies. Website hosting and Google Fonts receive connection information when you visit. We will update this notice if the services or technologies we use change.

Remaining confirmations

  • The business correspondence address and the website-hosting and email providers to name in the final notice.
  • Approval of the lawful bases above and confirmation that the retention criteria reflect the business’s actual requirements, including records held in ServiceM8, email and backups.
  • The providers’ processing roles, contractual arrangements, locations and any applicable international-transfer safeguards, including ServiceM8 and Resend.
